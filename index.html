About

I started out as a full-stack developer working with .NET Web API, Angular and SharePoint. Then I found the Power Platform, and it has been my main focus ever since. Simple apps led to enterprise solutions: multi-country approval systems, trade management platforms with custom code components and Azure Functions, and governance tooling that manages environments, licences and DLP policies at scale.

Today I lead architecture and delivery for multiple clients at Synapx. That means turning loose requirements into data models, integration patterns and app designs that teams can maintain long after go-live.

Outside client work I write, run a user group, speak at meetups and answer questions on the community forums. Technology does more good when it's shared.
